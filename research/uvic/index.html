RESEARCH 01

Operational Modal Analysis for Chatter Prediction in Turning

MASc research combining machining dynamics, vibration measurement, state-space modeling, signal processing, system identification, and stability analysis to estimate the approach to regenerative chatter while the process remains stable.

Non-contact vibration measurement using a Laser Doppler Vibrometer, acoustic excitation, MATLAB signal processing, and Operational Modal Analysis to identify micromilling-system dynamics.
