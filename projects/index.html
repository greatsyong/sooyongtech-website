Projects

My robotics projects are organized into three technical areas: Perception & Autonomy, Control Systems, and Reinforcement Learning. The work progresses from sensing and perception to model-based control, physical robotic implementation, learning-based adaptation, and engineering evaluation.

Perception & Autonomy

Robotic perception, multi-sensor integration, and autonomous path planning. The projects progress from extracting useful information from visual and range sensors to combining heterogeneous measurements and selecting practical planning strategies for navigation through complex environments.

Pre-Part 1 — UR5e Model Setup & Cross-Validation

A nominal UR5e analytical model is reconciled with the official ROS 2 robot description and NVIDIA Isaac Sim before controller design begins. DH and physical-link frames, fixed-base articulation, gravity, joint drives, link poses, Jacobians, the mass matrix, and gravity generalized forces are independently verified.
