Pre-Part 1 — UR5e Model Setup & Cross-Validation

A nominal UR5e analytical model is reconciled with the official ROS 2 robot description and NVIDIA Isaac Sim before controller design begins. DH and physical-link frames, fixed-base articulation, gravity, joint drives, link poses, Jacobians, the mass matrix, and gravity generalized forces are independently verified.

Part 1A — Kinematics & Dynamics Characterization

Characterization of the validated UR5e plant through Jacobian conditioning and singularity analysis, pseudoinverse versus damped least squares, configuration-dependent rigid-body dynamics, equilibrium and linearization studies, benchmark feasibility, and Python–C++ cross-validation before controller design.

Completed

Part 1B — Controller Design, Benchmarking & Robustness

PID, computed-torque, discrete LQR, and constrained MPC are evaluated on the validated nonlinear UR5e plant using a common benchmark. The study extends nominal tracking into computation, Python–C++ validation, persistent payload mismatch, and transient external-disturbance robustness.

Completed
PIDComputed TorqueLQR MPCRobustness

Part 2 — Isaac Sim Control Validation

PID, computed-torque, LQR, and MPC controllers developed in Part 1B are transferred to the UR5e in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. Analytical predictions are independently evaluated under physics simulation, with attention to tracking performance, actuator behavior, model discrepancy, and implementation effects.
