Engineering Approach

From physical assumptions to validated systems

I treat implementation, simulation, and numerical output as engineering evidence to be tested rather than results to be accepted at face value.

Model the physical problem

Define the system, assumptions, coordinate frames, states, constraints, and governing relationships before selecting algorithms or tuning parameters.

Implement and measure

Build working systems in ROS 2, Python, simulation, and robotics frameworks, then characterize their behavior using quantitative measurements rather than demonstration alone.

Cross-validate

Compare analytical calculations, numerical methods, simulation outputs, and physical expectations so that agreement is supported by independent evidence.

Diagnose before correcting

When results disagree, identify which assumptions, models, interfaces, or boundary conditions are responsible before modifying an implementation simply to obtain agreement.
